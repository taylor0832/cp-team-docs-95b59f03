What this means

We are not a company that is dying. We are a profitable services company carrying a product company that loses $22,484 every month. The group has ten months. Product, standing alone, has about six weeks. Those are two completely different problems and we have been solving the wrong one.

The date that is still real

Product holds $22,503. Holdings — its only outside funder — holds $7,119 after wiring $20,000 on 11 Aug. That is $29,622 against a $22,484/mo hole.

$29,622 ÷ $22,484 = 1.3 months → Product is unfunded on or about 20 SEPTEMBER 2026

After that, every product payroll is a decision to move Services' cash into Product. That is a choice we make deliberately, with a cap and an end date — or Product shrinks to fit its own revenue.
