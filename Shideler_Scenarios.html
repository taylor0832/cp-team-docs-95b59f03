2 · The three scenarios

Moving left to right is the entire strategy.

ScenarioWhat has to be trueYour position
A · The floorWe reach $6M of revenue, but the platform multiple is never earned: services trades like a services business, product like a small product business. A ~$51M company.~$4M + every commission check along the way
B · The thesisSame $6M of revenue, but margin reaches ~65% and product is half the mix, so the blended platform multiple (12–18×) is earned. A ~$90M company.~$7M (range $6–9M) + commissions
C · The barHaven/Delightree speed applied to our base: ~$19M of revenue by 2029, platform multiple held. A ~$230–285M company.~$18–23M + years of accelerator commissions

Margin and mix move us from A to B. Speed moves us from B to C. Your seat is the speed lever: the logos, the pipeline, the activation number. Which is the whole argument for why your equity concentrates where it does.
