02 — THE ONE CHANGE

The accountant network stops being free

The 21 July decision made accounting firms a distribution surface — "one distributes" — three to five firms onboarded free and scored on brand introductions. Charging them changes what it is.

Canon: firms as distribution

3–5 firms, free, measured on brand intros. Gate G3 was "one firm-sourced brand intro by 31 Oct." Revenue contribution: $0.

Now: firms as a revenue line

3 firms, $20/unit/mo plus 1% on payments, ~400 units. Revenue contribution: $13,284/mo, and they still make the brand introductions.

Why this is the Q3 plan working, not a departure from it

The canon's own words: "Deka's ~6-week single-brand baseline is why batches — brand-wide connects, firm book-connects — are the mechanism." Firm book-connects were already the named mechanism. The only change is that we charge for it.

And it clears the north star on its own: 330 named units takes us from 218 activated to ~550, past both the 300 target and the 400 stretch, without Swig.

79% of $2.0M, from $733K in July — that is $849K of net-new against the $1.27M the canon said we needed. It misses the number and it is still the best result available from here.

What turns base into stretch

Terry at 50% instead of 25%, new opens at the top of the canon range, and a 20% bookkeeping take on the accountant books. All three are services execution, not new logos.

The mix problem the raise will ask about

Even in the stretch case product is 22% of revenue, against a canon that wanted ~35%. Product reaches $410–446K, not $700K. We should say this plainly rather than let it surface in diligence: product got profitable instead of getting big, and profitable-at-$410K is a better story to walk into a raise with than burning at $700K.
