02 — THE MOTION

One motion, two revenue lines

Taylor sells accounting firms. Each firm that lands pays us twice — once in product, once in services — which is exactly why his cost can sit on Services while the wedge is a product.

Product line — the portal

Firms adopt the accountant workspace at $10/unit/mo. A firm keeping books for 45 franchise locations is $450/mo of product revenue that requires no franchisor deal and no new integration.

Kayden's read: "not perfect but good enough to sell."

Services line — the book

The same firms bring bookkeeping and CFO work, and refer brands. This is the line that has to replace the revenue Services is currently losing.

Jeff Kinney (~45 Deka locations) is first and already identified. Certus/Natalia, Luke and Lee are next.

Why this and not more outbound

Net-new booked 1–11 August was $5,400. Cold outbound is 0 replies on 57 delivered emails. One accounting firm carries a book of franchise clients in a single conversation — the same reason a franchisor deal beats a franchisee deal, applied one layer up.
