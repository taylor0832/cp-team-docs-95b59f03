02 — THE ECONOMICS

What an accountant unit is actually worth

The subscription is the smaller half. Payments are the bigger half, and that is the part worth getting right.

Deriving the payment number from our own volume

$290,416 succeeded over 2 weeks (PostHog) → $628,751/mo ÷ 238 paying units = $2,642/unit/mo

$2,642 × 1% = $26.42 per unit per month in ACH fee revenue

That is more than the $20 subscription. A franchise location running royalties and vendor payments through us is worth roughly $46/mo at full payment attach — and the fee scales with their sales without us doing anything.

The assumption most likely to be wrong — and it's yours to settle, Kayden

$26.42/unit is a network average. If the $628,751/mo of volume is concentrated in two or three large brands, then a typical accountant-channel franchisee moves far less and the ACH line is overstated.

The question: of the 238 paying units, how many actually process ACH, and what does the volume distribution look like? If the median unit is well below $2,642/mo, we re-cut this at the median rather than the mean. Nothing else in the model moves as much.

04 — THE MOTION

Three lanes, one seller

Lane 1 · Capture

$9,637/mo. Fee collection, base billing, and units inside brands that already pay us. Mostly Kayden's; no selling required.

Lane 2 · Accountant channel

$13,284/mo. Natalia, Certus, Franchise Resource. One conversation delivers a book of units instead of one location.

Lane 3 · Financial OS outbound

$4,207/mo. Outbound continues, aimed at brands. Zerorez first. Swig and the MindBody list are upside not in this model.

Why the accountant channel is the wedge and not just another lead source

A brand deal delivers units one franchisor at a time and takes months. An accounting firm already holds the book — Natalia alone is 130 units, which is more than every brand we have except DonutNV. The firm also does the work we would otherwise have to do: onboarding, data hygiene, chasing connections. We supply reports, proposals and payment rails; they supply distribution and fulfilment.

And it compounds with Lane 3 — a firm that likes the portal introduces us to the brands behind its clients.
